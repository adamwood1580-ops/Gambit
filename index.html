07 / KNOW BEFORE YOU PLAY◇

STRAIGHT
ANSWERS.

A useful idea still deserves careful questions.

Is the GAM presale open now?

No. This is a concept and development website. A presale contract is not connected, there are no purchase transactions here and no launch date is announced.

Does a 70% bonus mean I win 70% more often?

No. The proposed Angel rate adds 70% of eligible positive daily net profit in GAM, subject to published limits. It does not change the likelihood of a game outcome or guarantee any profit.

What happens if my day finishes at a loss?

No positive-profit bonus applies. Base gaming winnings and losses are accounted for independently from bonuses; eligible bonus amounts would settle only once per UTC day.

Can I use BNB or another token for the bonus?

The proposed daily bonus applies only to qualifying GAM-denominated play. Details of any other supported currencies or market products remain undecided.

Is 98% RTP included?

A distinct 98% RTP Angel game version was previously explored as a future concept. It is not a confirmed product setting and is not combined with the new daily net-profit rewards without mathematical and independent fairness review.

Are betting, casino and election markets all available?

No. These are proposed areas of development, dependent on permissions, reliable feeds, testing, age and eligibility controls, and clear settlement procedures. Some may not launch in all jurisdictions.

Can AI change odds for an individual player?

No adaptive, undisclosed odds manipulation is proposed. Statistical models may recommend market prices that are then validated; accepted bet odds are recorded and game randomness remains independently governed.
