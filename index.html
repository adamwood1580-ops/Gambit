08 / THE DETAILS◇

STRAIGHT
ANSWERS.

How GAM works, how the rewards are calculated and what wallet access means.

Can I buy GAM through this site today?

Not yet. The public presale price is set at $0.0001 per GAM with a cumulative $1,000 limit per wallet. Purchase and claim functions activate only when the verified on-chain presale contract is connected and the sale is open.

How does my founding tier work?

Your highest tier is based on the cumulative USD-equivalent value paid by that wallet during presale: Starter below $50, Bronze $50 to below $100, Silver $100 to below $500, Gold $500 to below $1,000 and Angel at $1,000. No purchase means no founding tier.

Does a 70% bonus mean I win more often?

No. The Angel rate adds 70% of eligible positive daily net GAM casino profit at reward settlement. It does not alter randomness or increase the probability of winning any individual game, and it does not guarantee a daily gain.

What counts as daily net profit?

Settled casino payouts in GAM minus settled casino stakes in GAM during the defined UTC reward day. If the result is negative or zero, no tier bonus is credited. Normal winnings are already in the gaming balance; only the bonus is an additional credit.

Can I finish playing before midnight?

Yes. You may close your reward day early and settle the eligible result once. Continuing to play afterward does not produce another bonus for that UTC day. Logging out is not itself a reward-settlement event.

Does my tier cover sportsbook or event bets?

No. The founding-tier daily-profit percentage described here applies to eligible GAM casino play. Sportsbook and event-market rewards, if offered, are separate programmes with their own rules.

Is the 98% RTP game included in the founding-tier formula?

No. RTP is a game-level theoretical average over large numbers of plays, not a personal win probability. The founding-tier benefit is the published daily net-profit bonus; any separately offered high-RTP game would have its own independently approved mathematics and terms.

Can Gambit AI change my accepted odds?

No. Validated statistical models may help set market prices before acceptance. Accepted odds are recorded for the bet; game outcomes are not secretly modified based on account history.
